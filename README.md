# Belote Academy - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Belote Academy mobile game ("the app", "we") collects, how it is
used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app and to the game
server they connect to.

## Information we collect

**Account information**

- If you sign up with an email and password: your email address and a one-way hash of your password. We
  never store your password itself.
- If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address and name, as shared by Google after you
  approve the sign-in. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
- If you play as a guest: a randomly generated guest ID. No email or name from outside the app is needed.

**Game profile and activity**

- The player name and avatar you choose.
- Game data: games played, wins and losses, level and experience, reputation, in-game coins, items you
  unlock or receive, and the tables and card decks you pick.
- Social features: your friends list, friend requests, players you block, and the ratings players give
  each other after a match.

**Information stored only on your device**

- Settings such as language, sound and music volume, and the gender you pick on first launch. These stay on
  your device and are not sent to our server.

**Technical information**

- Our server records basic request logs (for example, which address of the server was called and when,
  and the IP address of the connection) to keep the service running and secure.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
- To run online games, matchmaking, friends, leaderboards and the other features of the app.
- To show your player name, avatar, level, reputation and game results to other players where the game
  displays them.
- To prevent abuse, fix problems and keep the service working.

We do not sell your information and we do not use third-party analytics. The only ads in the app are
optional reward videos from Google AdMob, described under **Advertising**.

## Advertising

At the end of an online match the app offers a prize, and you can choose to watch a short video ad to
claim it or to double it. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch, and the app shows no other ads.

The videos come from Google AdMob. To show them, measure them and prevent ad fraud, Google may collect
information from your device, such as its advertising identifier (where your device allows it), IP
address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this under its
own policy: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
We do not receive this information, only totals such as how many ads were shown.

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form. You can
  consent, refuse, or choose purposes and partners one by one. If you refuse, you may still see ads that are
  not personalized. To answer the form again, clear the app's data or reinstall it.
- **Everywhere:** you can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization in your device
  settings (Android: Settings -> Google -> Ads, or Security & privacy -> Ads; iOS: Settings -> Privacy &
  Security -> Tracking).

## What other players can see

Your player name, avatar, level, reputation, ratings and game statistics are visible to other players
inside the game. Your email address is never shown to other players.

## Sharing

We share information only:

- With service providers that host the game server and its database (Amazon Web Services), who process it
  on our behalf.
- With Google, only to complete "Sign in with Google" when you choose it.
- With Google AdMob, when you choose to watch a reward video (see **Advertising**).
- When required by law.

## Purchases

The app has no real-money purchases. Coins, items and boosts are in-game only and cannot be bought or
cashed out with real money.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in
the app: **Settings -> Delete account**. This permanently removes your account and the data that belongs
to it, including your profile, items, purchases of in-game items, friends and ratings you received.
Ratings and reputation you gave other players stay as part of their totals. Deleted data may remain in
encrypted backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

Guest accounts can be deleted the same way.

## Security

Connections between the app and our server are encrypted (HTTPS / secure WebSocket). Passwords are stored
only as one-way hashes, and sign-in tokens are stored hashed on the server.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app
after a change means you accept the updated policy.
